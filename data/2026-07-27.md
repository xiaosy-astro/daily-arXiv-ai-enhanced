### [1] [A GRMHD-Calibrated Semi-Analytical Model for Hot Sub-Keplerian Accretion Flows in Kerr Spacetime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21852)
*Nabin Bhushal,Manil Khatiwada,Yogesh Maharjan,David Garofalo,Chandra B. Singh*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个半解析的运动学模型，通过插值开普勒轨道和自由落体解来描述克尔度规下的高温厚吸积流。模型使用平滑的径向过渡函数连接速度分量，无需显式磁场或应力项。经与磁 arrested盘（MAD）模拟数据对比验证，该模型能较好预测吸积流参数（角动量误差约1.2倍，速度/密度误差约1.6-1.8倍），比传统常数系数模型更精确，适用于射线追踪、参数探索和光谱建模等计算成本敏感的应用。

Motivation: 现有GRMHD模拟计算成本高，而传统常数系数半解析模型精度不足，因此需要一种计算高效且更精确的吸积流模型，以便广泛应用于射线追踪、参数扫描和光谱建模等场景。

Method: 基于克尔度规，利用径向平滑过渡函数T(r)将开普勒旋转解与自由落体解进行插值，构建半解析运动学模型。模型不含显式磁场，通过过渡函数实现流动特性随半径平滑变化。使用不同自旋参数（a=-0.9至+0.9）的MAD模拟数据对模型进行校准和验证。

Result: 模型预测的径向速度、角速度和密度剖面与MAD模拟数据的平均误差分别约为1.8、1.6和1.6倍，比角动量的预测误差（约1.2倍）略大。整体精度显著优于传统常数系数模型。

Conclusion: 该半解析运动学模型在计算效率和预测精度之间取得了良好平衡，是GRMHD模拟的高效替代方案，可用于射线追踪、吸积参数探索和光谱建模等应用。

Abstract: We develop a simple, semi-analytical, kinematic model for hot, thick accretion flows, constructed by interpolating between Keplerian and free-fall geodesic solutions in the Kerr metric. Unlike self-consistent general relativistic magnetohydrodynamics (GRMHD) frameworks, our model contains no explicit magnetic fields or stress terms; instead, it uses a smooth, radially varying transition function T(r) to connect the velocity components from near-Keplerian rotation at large distances to a free-fall state near the event horizon. While the coefficients $α$ and $β$ remain constant, the transition function is a true function of radius, allowing the flow properties to vary smoothly with radius. We calibrate and validate this model against time- and azimuthally averaged profiles from long-duration magnetically arrested disk (MAD) simulations spanning a wide range of black hole spins ($a=-0.9$ to $+0.9$). The model successfully captures the properties of accretion flow parameters across both prograde and retrograde configurations. Quantitatively, the predicted radial velocity, angular velocity, and density profiles match the simulation data to within an average factor of approximately 1.8, 1.6, and 1.6, respectively, while the specific angular momentum exhibits the closest agreement, remaining within a factor of approximately 1.2. This fast semi-analytical prescription gives significantly lower errors than previous constant-coefficient models and it is a computationally affordable tool for various applications such as ray tracing, accretion parameter exploration, and spectral modelling.
