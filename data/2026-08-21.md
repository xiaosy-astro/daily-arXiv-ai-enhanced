### [1] [High-Redshift Type Ia Supernovae Exhibit Enhanced Calcium Abundances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18342)
*Xingzhuo Chen,Ulisses Braga-Neto,Lifan Wang*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该研究利用AI辅助反演技术分析了来自超新星遗留巡天（Supernova Legacy Survey）的高红移Ia型超新星以及詹姆斯·韦伯太空望远镜观测的引力透镜Ia型超新星光谱，将样本扩展至红移2.05。研究发现Ia型超新星钙丰度与红移呈正相关，且这种红移依赖的钙丰度变化超出了不同前身星金属丰度的核合成模拟预测，表明高红移Ia型超新星可能与邻近的Ia型超新星经历不同的爆炸机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Ia型超新星是宇宙化学增丰的主要贡献者，其元素丰度可作为探测前身星性质和爆炸物理的探针。作者希望通过分析高红移样本（包括韦伯望远镜观测的引力透镜超新星）来研究宇宙学时间尺度上Ia型超新星性质的演化。

Method: 采用人工智能辅助反演技术，对高红移SNe Ia光谱进行元素丰度反演。数据来源包括Supernova Legacy Survey的高红移样本和JWST观测的引力透镜SNe Ia，样本扩展至红移2.05。

Result: 发现SN Ia钙丰度与红移呈正相关。红移依赖的钙丰度变化幅度超过SN Ia核合成模拟（改变前身星金属丰度）的预测范围。

Conclusion: 高红移SNe Ia可能采用了与邻近SNe Ia不同的爆炸机制，即钙丰度的红移演化无法仅由前身星金属丰度差异解释。

Abstract: Type Ia supernovae (SNe Ia) are major contributors to cosmic chemical enrichment, and their elemental abundances provide a probe of progenitor properties and explosion physics across cosmic time. We employ an artificial intelligence-assisted inversion technique to analyze spectra of high-redshift SNe Ia from the Supernova Legacy Survey and of gravitationally lensed SNe Ia observed by the James Webb Space Telescope, extending the sample to redshift 2.05. We find a positive correlation between SN Ia calcium abundance and redshift. The redshift-dependent variation in calcium abundance exceeds that predicted by SN Ia nucleosynthesis simulations with varying progenitor metallicities, suggesting that high-redshift SNe Ia may undergo different explosion mechanisms from nearby SNe Ia.

### [2] [Modeling the Gravitational Wave Signal from the X-ray Pulsar Her X-1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18343)
*I. D. Markozov,G. K. Pimenov,A. A. Mushtukov*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文针对X射线脉冲星Her X-1的引力波信号进行建模，将中子星视为自由进动的椭球体，在四极近似下计算引力波信号，构建了h×和h+偏振轮廓，评估了DECIGO望远镜的探测可能性，并推导了基于小椭率参数微扰法的半解析建模公式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究动机是对Her X-1这类X射线脉冲星产生的引力波信号进行理论建模，以便评估未来引力波探测器（如DECIGO）对该类源的可探测性，并建立可用于实际数据处理的理论模板。

Method: 将中子星建模为自由进动的椭球体，在四极近似下计算引力波信号；利用当前文献中测量的Her X-1中子星进动参数构建h×和h+偏振轮廓；通过小椭率参数的微扰方法推导半解析建模公式。

Result: 构建了Her X-1中子星在四极近似下的引力波偏振信号轮廓，基于实测进动参数进行了具体计算，并评估了DECIGO望远镜对该信号的探测可行性。

Conclusion: 论文提供了对进动中子星引力波信号的半解析建模方法，为未来DECIGO等空间引力波探测器对Her X-1类源的探测与参数估计提供了理论基础和计算工具。

Abstract: The paper is devoted to modeling the gravitational waves from the X-ray pulsar Her X-1. The neutron star is considered as a freely precessing ellipsoid. The gravitational wave signal is calculated in the quadrupole approximation. The $h_{\times}$ and $h_{+}$ polarization profiles are constructed for the precession parameters of the neutron star in Her X-1 measured in up-to-date works. The possibility of signal detection with the DECIGO telescope is assessed. The formulas for the semi-analytical modeling of the gravitational wave signal from precessing neutron stars by the method of perturbations in the small neutron star ellipticity parameter are deduced.

</details>
