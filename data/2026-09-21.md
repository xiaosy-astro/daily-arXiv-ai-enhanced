### [1] [A method for enhanced gamma-proton discrimination with imaging atmospheric Cherenkov telescopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21635)
*Jianling Liu,Hu Liu*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该文提出基于切伦科夫光子发射位置横向分布的新观测量，结合似然比方法进行伽马-质子鉴别，在1 TeV附近将背景拒绝能力提升约一个数量级。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 甚高能伽马射线天文的关键技术挑战是有效区分伽马光子与背景（主要是质子）。星载量能器在1 TeV附近可达10^4-10^5的拒绝能力，而地面成像切伦科夫望远镜仅约10，存在很大优化空间。

Method: 使用CORSIKA和sim_telarray模拟H.E.S.S.探测器响应，从切伦科夫簇射图像重建光子发射位置；借鉴星载量能器分析技术，构造基于切伦科夫光子发射位置横向分布的新观测量，并采用似然比方法融合多望远镜的单镜变量，与基于Hillas的传统分析对比。

Result: 基于光子发射位置横向分布的观测量对切伦科夫图像尺寸依赖性更弱，单镜伽马-质子鉴别优于Hillas宽度参数；似然比结合width方法显著优于常规的mean reduced scaled width，1 TeV附近提升约一个数量级，10 TeV以上仍超过50%。

Conclusion: 借鉴星载量能器思想构造的切伦科夫发射位置横向分布观测量，结合似然比多镜融合，可大幅提升地面切伦科夫望远镜的伽马-质子鉴别能力，为甚高能伽马探测提供新优化方向。

Abstract: Purpose: Very-high-energy gamma-ray astronomy is an important window to study the extreme astrophysical processes in the universe, and how to effectively distinguish between gamma photons and background signals (mainly protons) is a key technical challenge to realize very-high-energy gamma-ray detection. Spaceborne calorimeters record secondary particle spatial distributions, achieving a background rejection power of 10000-100000 near 1 TeV. Ground-based imaging Cherenkov telescopes measure Cherenkov photon angular distributions, yet they only reach a rejection factor of about 10 in this energy range, leaving substantial room for optimization. Methods: We use CORSIKA and sim_telarray to simulate H.E.S.S. detector responses. Photon emission positions are reconstructed from Cherenkov shower images. Inspired by analysis techniques for space-borne calorimeter, we propose new observables derived from the transverse distribution of Cherenkov photon emission positions to separate gamma-ray signals from proton backgrounds. We employ a likelihood-ratio method to combine single-telescope variables across multiple telescopes and compare its performance with conventional Hillas-based analysis. Results: The results show that observables built from the transverse distribution of Cherenkov photon emission positions are less sensitive to Cherenkov image size and outperform the Hillas width parameter for single-telescope gamma-proton discrimination. Furthermore, the likelihood ratio combining width yields substantially better performance than the conventional mean reduced scaled width: the improvement reaches roughly one order of magnitude near 1 TeV, and this enhancement remains above 50% for energies above 10 TeV.

### [2] [Wave-particle acceleration during supernova explosions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21652)
*Zaza N. Osmanov,S. M. Mahajan*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文研究超新星爆发早期通过电磁波与量子波共振相互作用加速粒子的机制，发现可将近质子加速至约3×10^14 eV，且粒子能量随光度下降先升后降。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探讨超新星爆发早期是否存在有效机制将粒子加速到高能（如宇宙线能量尺度），并厘清加速过程与光度演化之间的关系。

Method: 采用电磁波与粒子量子波之间的共振相互作用模型，针对典型前身星质量(8、12、20倍太阳质量)进行数值/解析分析。

Result: 对典型前身星质量，该机制可将质子加速至最高约3×10^14 eV；尽管光度持续下降，粒子能量先增大、达到最大值后再减小。

Conclusion: 共振加速机制在超新星早期能有效产生高能质子，其能量演化特征反映了共振加速机制的典型行为。

Abstract: We investigate particle acceleration during the early stages of a supernova explosion via a resonant interaction between electromagnetic waves and quantum waves associated with particles. For typical progenitor masses of $(8; 12; 20)\times M_s$, we find that this mechanism can accelerate protons to energies of up to $3\times 10^{14}$ eV. Interestingly, despite the continuously declining luminosity, the particle energy initially increases, reaches a maximum, and subsequently decreases, reflecting the characteristic behavior of the resonant acceleration mechanism.

</details>
