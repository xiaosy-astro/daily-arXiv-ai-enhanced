### [1] [Probing AU-Scale Magnetic-Field Reversals in the Interstellar Medium with Pulsar Scintillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22728)
*Jacob Yen,Daniel Baker,Dongzi Li,Ue-Li Pen*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文提出一种利用相位恢复技术从脉冲星偏振数据中提取双折射引起的极化信号的方法，以探测AU尺度星际介质中的磁场反转（电流片）。通过对PSR B0834+06的档案观测数据演示，成功将1-ms散射特征在Doppler-delay空间中分解为两个分支，测得分支间的旋转测度差为(-9.3±3.2)×10⁻³ rad m⁻²（2.9σ），与约4.4±2.1 μG的磁场反转一致。作者将其定位为概念验证，而非确定性检测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AU尺度星际介质结构对脉冲星计时阵列和宇宙线传播至关重要。现有方法中，脉冲星闪烁能分辨这些结构但无法探测其磁场构型；传统法拉第旋转测度（RM）在AU尺度上灵敏度不足且受电离层系统误差影响。因此需要新方法同时解决空间分辨率和磁场探测两个问题。

Method: 将相位恢复（phase retrieval）应用于极化数据，分离出双折射引起的极化信号——磁场反转的特征标志。利用脉冲星闪烁将光线路径分辨至~1 AU尺度，实现差分RM测量，从而消除电离层共模噪声并保留AU尺度磁场信号。在Doppler-delay空间中通过相位恢复将散射特征分解为两个分支，对应电流片两侧的射线路径。

Result: 在PSR B0834+06的档案观测中，相位恢复成功将1-ms散射特征分解为两个Doppler-delay分支。测得分支间RM差为(-9.3±3.2)×10⁻³ rad m⁻²（2.9σ，跨10-MHz带宽），与|Δ⟨B∥⟩|≈4.4±2.1 μG的磁场反转一致，足以维持长寿命电流片。

Conclusion: 该方法是概念验证而非确定性检测（仅6个独立子带）。若可扩展，全实现版将能约束宇宙线输运并为脉冲星计时阵列中的传播诱导噪声建模提供信息。

Abstract: AU-scale structures in the interstellar medium have become relevant across several areas of astrophysics, from ISM microphysics to pulsar timing arrays and cosmic-ray (CR) propagation. Current sheets formed at magnetic-field reversals have drawn particular attention because they can deflect the dominant GeV CR population through large angles. Pulsar scintillation, an outstanding probe of plasma density, resolves these structures---but not their magnetic configuration. Conventional Faraday rotation measures (RM) are only marginally sensitive at AU scales and are further compromised by ionospheric systematics.
  We propose a method that isolates birefringence-induced polarization---the signature of magnetic reversal---by applying phase retrieval to polarized data. Pulsar scintillation further resolves ray paths separated by $\sim 1\,\rm{AU}$, enabling differential RM measurements that suppress the ionospheric common mode while preserving the AU-scale magnetic signal. We demonstrate the method on an archival observation of PSR B0834+06. Phase retrieval resolves the 1-ms scattering feature into two branches in Doppler-delay space. Under the corrugated current-sheet interpretation, these branches correspond to ray paths sampling opposite sides of an AU-scale current sheet. We measure a branch-to-branch RM difference of $(-9.3\pm3.2)\times 10^{-3}\text{ rad m}^{-2}$ at 2.9$σ$ across a 10-MHz band. The signal is consistent with a field reversal of $|Δ\langle B_\parallel \rangle| \simeq4.4\pm2.1\,μ\rm G$, sufficient to sustain a long-lived sheet. With only six independent subbands, we present this as a proof of concept rather than a definitive detection. If it proves scalable, its full implementation could constrain cosmic-ray transport and inform models of propagation-induced noise in pulsar timing arrays.

### [2] [Tidal disruption of stellar binaries as a pathway to exotic transients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22735)
*Mauricio González-Servín,Emilio Tejeda,Susana Lizano,Luis A. Manzaneda,Raúl Cano-Villegas*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文通过受限三体动力学和SPH模拟，研究了恒星双星（太阳类似星+白矮星）被超大质量黑洞潮汐分离的机制，发现该机制能自然产生偏心潮汐瓦解事件（TDEs），分为椭圆型TDE（eTDE）和双曲型TDE（hTDE）两类，各占50%概率。约88%的轨道取向产生干净瓦解，约12%的白矮星会俘获物质形成带碎片包层的白矮星（WDDE），其中约4%涉及白矮星与太阳类似星的直接碰撞，导致吸积回退峰值时间提前5倍、峰值高度提高20倍。该机制还与重复部分TDE和准周期爆发（QPEs）相关，为多样化TDE观测提供了稳健的产生渠道。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统TDE前身星模型通常假设单星沿抛物线轨道绕超大质量黑洞运行，但观测显示TDE的动力学路径更加多样化。作者旨在探索恒星双星被黑洞潮汐分离这一机制，以解释观测中更丰富的TDE类型和特征。

Method: 作者使用受限三体动力学分析和光滑粒子流体动力学（SPH）模拟，构建了由太阳类似星（SLS）和白矮星（WD）组成的双星系统，使它们沿抛物线轨道围绕10^6 M_sun的超大质量黑洞运动，系统研究了不同双星轨道相位对演化结果的影响。

Result: 发现双星轨道相位决定结果：一个组分被捕获到紧密束缚轨道，另一个被弹射为超高速天体。产生的TDE分为eTDE和hTDE两类，概率相等；约88%为干净瓦解，约12%形成WDDE（其中约4%涉及直接碰撞导致回退显著增强，约1.5%的WDDE总质量超过1.4 M_sun），还可产生rTDEs和QPEs。

Conclusion: 双星与SMBH的相遇为产生具有不同观测特征的多样化TDE提供了稳健的物理渠道，可以解释从类新星事件到奇特红巨星状天体的多种观测结果。

Abstract: Tidal disruption event (TDE) progenitors are commonly modelled as single stars on parabolic orbits around a supermassive black hole (SMBH), yet observations point to a richer diversity of dynamical pathways. We show that the tidal separation of stellar binaries by a $10^6\,M_\odot$ SMBH provides a natural mechanism for producing eccentric TDEs. Using restricted three-body dynamics and smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) simulations, we model a binary composed of a solar-like star (SLS) and a white dwarf (WD) on a parabolic orbit. The binary orbital phase governs the outcome: one component is captured onto a tightly bound orbit while the other is ejected as a hypervelocity object, naturally producing TDEs with eccentricities $e \neq 1$. We classify the resulting events into Elliptical TDEs (eTDEs) and Hyperbolic TDEs (hTDEs), which occur with equal probability. For $\sim 88\%$ of binary orientations the disruption is clean, with no mass accreted by the WD. The remaining $\sim 12\%$ lies in two narrow windows of binary phase in which the WD captures material and becomes a WD with debris envelope (WDDE). About a third of that range, $\sim 4\%$ of all orientations, involves a direct WD--SLS collision near pericenter, giving fallback that peaks up to five times earlier and twenty times higher than in the single-star case; for the innermost $\sim 1.5\%$ the total WDDE mass exceeds $1.4\,M_\odot$, although the degenerate core itself does not, since the captured material forms a non-degenerate envelope. This suggests outcomes ranging from nova-like events to peculiar red giant-like objects. Depending on the binary phase, the mechanism may also produce repeating partial TDEs (rTDEs) and quasi-periodic eruptions (QPEs). Binary--SMBH encounters thus provide a robust channel for generating diverse TDEs with distinct observational signatures.

### [3] [Timing, Polarization, and Single-Pulse Properties of Long-Period FAST Pulsars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22771)
*Habtamu Menberu Tedila,Shijun Dang,Di Li,Pei Wang,Jianping Yuan,Rai Yuen,Na Wang,Shakhboz Khasanov*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文对两颗FAST-CRAFTS长周期脉冲星（PSR J0000+6252和J2131+3642）进行了相位连接的计时和偏振测量，并将单脉冲发射态分析扩展到包含五颗源的样本。研究测量了基本参数（自转周期、周期导数、特征年龄、表面磁场、自转减慢光度），并用高斯混合模型（GMM）识别了不同脉冲星的弱态、爆发态和无效态等单脉冲发射成分，还发现了亮单脉冲（峰值强度≥10倍平均轮廓）及其与正常射电发射束的关系。结果突显了缓慢旋转中子星磁层变率的多样性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 长周期脉冲星是研究射电辐射机制和磁层物理的重要源，但这类源的样本相对有限。通过FAST的高灵敏度观测，可以对长周期脉冲星进行精确的计时和偏振测量，并通过单脉冲发射态分析揭示其磁层变率的多样性，从而加深对中子星辐射物理的理解。

Method: 1) 利用FAST L波段观测进行相位连接的计时分析，时间跨度为414-511天；2) 对两颗脉冲星进行偏振测量，包括旋转矢量模型（RVM）拟合；3) 对五颗脉冲星的单脉冲能量分布进行高斯混合模型（GMM）聚类分析，识别不同的发射态成分（无效态、弱态、爆发态）；4) 识别亮单脉冲（峰值强度≥10倍平均积分轮廓），并测量散射时标。

Result: 测得两颗脉冲星的自转周期为1.11-1.55 s，特征年龄4.34-6.16 Myr，表面磁场(2.15-2.52)×10^12 G。PSR J2131+3642的旋转测量为-44.6±7.6 rad m^-2并支持RVM拟合，J0000+6252偏振点太少无法拟合。GMM识别出四颗源具有无效/弱/爆发态，J2131+3642仅呈现弱态和爆发态。发现亮单脉冲数量分布不同（J0000+6252有42个，J1903+1407和J2112+4058各4个），均出现在主发射窗口内且无周期重复，表明是正常射电束的零散增强。J2112+4058散射时标为5.84±0.18 ms。

Conclusion: 这些长周期脉冲星展现出多样化的磁层变率行为：不同源具有不同的单脉冲发射态组合和亮脉冲活动水平，表明缓慢旋转的中子星的射电发射机制存在显著的个体差异。亮单脉冲与正常发射窗口一致且无周期性，支持其为常规发射束的零散增强而非分离的巨脉冲现象。

Abstract: We present phase-connected timing and polarization measurements for two long-period FAST-CRAFTS pulsars, PSRs J0000+6252 and J2131+3642, and extend single-pulse emission-state analysis to a five-source sample including PSRs J1903+1407, J1502+4653, and J2112+4058. FAST L-band timing baselines span 414 to 511 days; the two pulsars have spin periods of 1.11 to 1.55 s, period derivatives of $(3.99$ to $4.06)\times10^{-15}~{\rm s~s^{-1}}$, characteristic ages of 4.34 to 6.16 Myr, surface magnetic fields of $(2.15$ to $2.52)\times10^{12}$ G, and spin-down luminosities of $(4.21\times10^{31}$ to $1.16\times10^{32})~{\rm erg~s^{-1}}$. Their rotation measures are $70.2\pm26.7$ and $-44.6\pm7.6~{\rm rad~m^{-2}}$, with linear polarization fractions of 17.6\% to 27.2\%. PSR J2131+3642 shows a short monotonic PA segment permitting a formal rotating-vector-model fit, though limited longitude coverage leaves the geometric parameters poorly constrained; PSR J0000+6252 has too few PA points for such a fit. Gaussian mixture modeling (GMM) of single-pulse energy distributions identifies null, weak, and burst components in PSRs J0000+6252, J1903+1407, J1502+4653, and J2112+4058, while J2131+3642 shows only weak and burst states. We also identify bright single pulses (peak intensity $\geq10\times$ the integrated average profile): 42 in J0000+6252, four each in J1903+1407 and J2112+4058, and none in J2131+3642 or J1502+4653. These bright pulses occur within the main emission window with no evidence of periodic recurrence, consistent with sporadic enhancements of the normal radio-emission beam. For J2112+4058, we measure a scattering timescale $τ_{\rm sc}=5.84\pm0.18$ ms at $ν_{\rm ref}=1.25$ GHz. Together, these results highlight the diversity of magnetospheric variability among slowly rotating neutron stars.

</details>
