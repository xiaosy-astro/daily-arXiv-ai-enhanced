### [1] [Comparing the size evolution of GRB 221009A with afterglow models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02988)
*S. Giarratana,O. S. Salafia,B. Marcote,Z. Paragi,M. Giroletti,G. Ghirlanda,L. Nava,A. van der Horst,J. K. Leung,L. Rhodes,P. G. Edwards,M. Pérez-Torres,J. Yang*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文利用全球VLBI网在5 GHz对GRB221009A在爆发后231和750天进行观测，测得亚毫角秒尺寸并拟合其时间演化，得到幂律指数a≈0.52。模型给出喷流能量与介质密度的约束，且发现现有文献中基于标准余辉模型的光变/光谱拟合参数无法在2σ内与本工作一致，其预测尺寸演化也与观测不符。强调尺寸测量是独立的动力学探针。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: GRB余辉通常通过光变曲线和光谱来约束动力学与结构，但存在参数简并，难以独立检验标准余辉模型。尺寸的直接测量可提供独立约束，检验现有文献参数与预测。

Method: 使用全球VLBI网在5 GHz对GRB221009A在爆发后231–750天进行观测，测量亚毫角秒半高全宽；结合文献中5和8 GHz尺寸数据，用单幂律拟合时间演化；再用公开代码VegasAfterglow建模正向激波在均匀ISM或风状介质中的减速过程。

Result: 测得尺寸演化幂律斜率a=0.52(+0.14/-0.12)；模型约束：ISM情形log[(E_jet/n) erg^-1 cm^-3]=54.2(+0.4/-0.5)，风情形log[(E_jet/A_*)]=51.7(+0.4/-0.2)。文献中可被VegasAfterglow复现的标准余辉模型最佳拟合参数无一在2σ内符合这些约束，且其预测尺寸演化与观测不符。

Conclusion: 余辉尺寸测量是独立的动力学、结构与辐射机制探针，能有效检验并挑战现有标准余辉模型对GRB221009A的解释。

Abstract: We present very long baseline interferometry (VLBI) observations of GRB221009A obtained with the global-VLBI network at 5 GHz between 231 and 750 days after the burst. The quality of our observations allowed us to measure the sub-milliarcsecond full-width at half maximum of the source surface brightness distribution with a one-sigma equivalent error of 13% and 30% at 231 and 391 days post-burst, respectively. By fitting the temporal evolution of these new size measurements alongside those previously published in the literature at 5 and 8 GHz with a single power law $\propto t^a$, we find a slope of $a = 0.52^{+0.14}_{-0.12}$. Using the public code VegasAfterglow, we model the 5 GHz size evolution considering the afterglow emission of the forward shock produced by a jet decelerating in either a homogeneous interstellar medium (ISM) or into a surrounding medium with a wind-like density profile. The model constrains the collimation-corrected Sedov ratio, that is, the ratio of the jet kinetic energy $E_\mathrm{jet}$ to the density of the surrounding medium ($n$ in the ISM scenario, or $A_\star$ in the wind scenario), in the range $\log[(E_\mathrm{jet}/n)\,\mathrm{erg}^{-1}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}] = 54.2^{+0.4}_{-0.5}$ for the ISM scenario and $\log[(E_\mathrm{jet}/A_\star)\,\mathrm{erg}^{-1}] = 51.7^{+0.4}_{-0.2}$ for the wind scenario. Among the works in the literature that fit the light curves and spectra of GRB221009A based on the standard afterglow model, and which can be reproduced with VegasAfterglow, none has best-fitting parameters that are compatible with these constraints within $2σ$. Moreover, we show that their predicted size evolution is not compatible with the observed one. Our results highlight the importance of size measurements of GRB afterglows as an independent probe of their dynamics, structure, and emission mechanisms.

### [2] [Twins in the Shadow: Phase Transitions in Proto-Neutron Stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03148)
*Jannik Milthaler,Jan-Erik Christian,Pia Jakobus,Stephan Rosswog*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文研究强子→退禁闭夸克物质的一阶相变对原中子星演化的影响，发现双子星只有在杂化星不具备重子孪星时才可能形成，这排除了大多数孪星对。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测"孪星"（同质量不同半径的中子星）被视为相变的决定性证据，但需检验原中子星演化是否允许这类孪星存在。

Method: 构建高度可参数化的状态方程：强子物质用相对论平均场模型，夸克物质用常声速模型；将原中子星冷却近似为固定重子质量下熵递减的序列。

Result: 某些参数化给出双子星，但原中子星演化表明引力孪星仅在杂化星不同时具备重子孪星时才可能；若相变发生在高密度则大多数孪星对被排除，未占据的杂化支在质量-半径图中"藏于"强子支的阴影里。

Conclusion: 原中子星演化强烈约束孪星的存在，显著减少可观测的孪星对，削弱其作为相变"烟枪信号"的可靠性。

Abstract: We study the impact of a first-order phase transition from hadronic to deconfined quark matter on the evolution of proto-neutron stars. To maximize the applicability of our results, we construct an equation of state that is highly parameterizable, where we use a relativistic mean field approach to model hadronic matter and a constant speed of sound approach to model quark matter. We approximate the cooling behavior of proto-neutron stars as a sequence of decreasing entropy at constant baryonic mass. Some parameterizations lead to so-called "twin-stars", where two neutron stars have the same (gravitational) mass but different radii. Detecting them is regarded as a smoking gun signal for phase transition. We find, however, that proto-neutron star evolution suggests that gravitational twin stars are only possible if the hybrid star does not have, in addition, a baryonic twin. This rules out most twin star pairs, especially if the phase transition occurs at large densities. In a mass-radius diagram the unpopulated hybrid branch appears to be "in the shadow" of the hadronic branch.

### [3] [Guide field effects on particle acceleration in three-dimensional relativistic magnetic reconnection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03427)
*Despina Karavola,Lorenzo Sironi,Maria Petropoulou*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该文通过3D相对论对等离子体磁重联模拟，研究导引场强度对粒子加速的影响，发现快加速通道在3D中持续存在但随导引场增强而效率降低。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近期3D PIC模拟表明弱导引场下粒子可近乎以最大速率加速；本文探究当导引场可与重联场相比时该快加速通道是否仍然存在。

Method: 进行3D相对论对等离子体磁重联模拟，并辅以2D对应模拟，在重联出流方向采用开放边界以建立准稳态。

Result: 导引场增强会降低重联率，并使高能粒子更紧密地沿局域磁场排列；3D中快加速通道（2D中不存在）在全导引场范围内持续存在，但随导引场增强涉及粒子比例逐渐减小；更强导引场产生更陡的能谱和更低的最大洛伦兹因子。

Conclusion: 导引场强度是调控相对论磁重联中粒子加速效率与能谱硬度的关键参数；3D效应对于快加速通道不可或缺。

Abstract: Relativistic magnetic reconnection is a key mechanism for particle acceleration in astrophysical plasmas. Recent 3D particle-in-cell simulations have shown that, for weak guide fields, particles can be accelerated at nearly the maximal rate by escaping the reconnected plasma and gaining energy from the large-scale upstream electric field. Here, we investigate whether this fast acceleration channel persists as the guide field becomes comparable to the reconnecting field. We perform 3D simulations of relativistic pair-plasma reconnection, complemented by corresponding 2D runs, using open boundaries along the reconnection outflows to establish a quasi-steady state. We find that increasing the guide field strength reduces the reconnection rate and causes energetic particles to become more closely aligned with the local magnetic field. In 3D, the fast acceleration channel---absent in 2D---persists across the full range of guide fields investigated, but involves a progressively smaller fraction of particles as the guide field increases. Consequently, stronger guide fields produce steeper particle energy spectra and lower maximum Lorentz factors.

### [4] [Forward Bayesian Inference for the Binary Black Hole Populations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03459)
*Poulami Dutta Roy,Tanmaya Mishra,Achal Kumar,Sergey Klimenko*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文提出 PopCWB，一个基于无模型搜索管道 cWB 的双黑洞族群推断贝叶斯框架。它采用正向方法，用 cWB 重构的模拟事件及其机器学习回归估计的总质量构建边际概率分布，从而构造似然。应用于 O3 数据，得到恒星演化最大黑洞质量约 45.5 M☉ 及本地并合率约 23.1 Gpc⁻³yr⁻¹。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准族群分析采用逆向方法，依赖事件级后验样本。本文希望利用无模型搜索管道 cWB 直接对族群进行推断，避免对事件级后验的依赖，提供一种正向的族群推断途径。

Method: PopCWB 采用正向方法：改变族群模型以找出最能描述观测事件的最优模型。不使用事件级后验样本，而是使用 cWB 重构的大量模拟事件及其通过机器学习回归估计的总质量，构建边际概率分布，并据此构造似然函数。

Result: 应用于 O3 期间 cWB 检测的 BBH 事件，约束了包含脉冲对不稳定超新星和动力学并合效应的天体物理族群模型。预测恒星演化产生的最大黑洞质量为 45.5^{+5.3}_{-6.5} M☉，推断本地 BBH 并合率为 23.1^{+11.3}_{-8.4} Gpc⁻³yr⁻¹。结果与现有文献族群模型进行了比较。

Conclusion: PopCWB 提供了一种基于 cWB 的正向贝叶斯族群推断框架，并成功应用于 O3 双黑洞数据，给出了最大黑洞质量与本地并合率的约束，与文献结果具有可比性。

Abstract: We present PopCWB, a Bayesian framework for the inference of the binary black hole (BBH) population based on the unmodeled search pipeline coherent WaveBurst (cWB). The standard population analysis takes an inverse approach by inferring the underlying population model parameters for a given set of detected events using the individual posterior samples. In PopCWB, we take a forward approach to population inference by varying population models to identify the most optimal one which describes the observed events. Rather than using event-level posterior samples, PopCWB uses a large set of simulated events reconstructed by cWB and their total mass, estimated with a machine-learning regression, to construct marginal probability distributions. These marginal probabilities are then used in the construction of the likelihood. We apply PopCWB to BBH events detected by cWB during the third observing run (O3) of the LIGO--Virgo--KAGRA detectors. We constrain an astrophysically motivated BBH population model that incorporates the effects of pulsational pair-instability supernovae and dynamical mergers. The analysis predicts a maximum black hole mass of $\mathbf{45.5^{+5.3}_{-6.5} M_{\odot}}$ from stellar evolution and an inferred local BBH merger rate of $\mathbf{23.1^{+11.3}_{-8.4} \, Gpc^{-3} yr^{-1}}$. We compare these results with the existing population models from the literature.
