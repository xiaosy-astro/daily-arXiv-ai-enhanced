### [1] [Extreme mass ratio inspirals from the Hills mechanism, The eccentricity distribution of Hills EMRIs in LISA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13294)
*Biancamaria Sersante,Davide Mancieri,Kevin Leopoldo Muñoz Zavala,Luca Broggi,Zephyr Penoyre,Elena Maria Rossi,Alberto Sesana*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文首次对Hills机制产生EMRI进行自洽建模，发现Hills EMRI比经典EMRI圆得多，为LISA识别形成通道提供依据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: EMRI是未来空间引力波探测器的关键波源，但Hills机制（双黑洞被MBH潮汐分离）这一形成通道此前缺乏自洽建模，其EMRI性质与经典两体弛豫通道的差异不明。

Method: 结合核星团轨道平均Fokker-Planck处理、双星-MBH三体相遇建模、被俘BH的Monte Carlo演化，以及完全相对论EMRI轨迹，追踪从双星供给到最终坠落的演化。

Result: Hills EMRI远较经典EMRI圆：对4×10^6 M☉ MBH、20 M☉圆轨道等质量BBH（0.1 AU），LISA波段进入平均偏心率约0.097、坠落时0.034，经典则分别为0.478和0.228；约0.4%初始BBH变成EMRI，而被俘BH约60%成为EMRI；Hills EMRI产率约1.6×10^-8/年/星系，约为经典EMRI的12%。

Conclusion: Hills通道对整体偏心率分布的贡献强烈依赖NSC中双星质量占比，对LISA识别EMRI形成通道及准周期爆发研究有重要启示。

Abstract: Extreme mass ratio inspirals (EMRIs) are promising sources for future space-based gravitational-wave detectors. We investigate EMRIs produced through the Hills mechanism, in which a stellar-mass black-hole binary (BBH) is tidally separated by a massive black hole (MBH), leaving one component bound to it. We present the first self-consistent modelling of this channel, combining an orbit-averaged Fokker-Planck treatment of the nuclear star cluster (NSC), three-body modelling of the binary-MBH encounter, Monte Carlo evolution of captured black holes (BHs), and fully relativistic EMRI trajectories to follow the evolution from binary supply down to final plunge. We find that Hills EMRIs are substantially more circular than classical EMRIs formed through two-body relaxation. For an MBH of $4 \times 10^6 M_\odot$ and circular, equal-mass BBHs of $20 M_\odot$ with initial separation of $0.1$ AU, EMRI mean eccentricities are approximately $0.097$ at our adopted LISA-band entry threshold and $0.034$ at plunge, compared with $0.478$ and $0.228$, respectively, for classical EMRIs. Of the initial BBHs, approximately $0.4$ per cent become EMRIs, whereas approximately $60$ per cent of captured BHs do. For comparable NSC initial compact-object mass, we find approximately $1.6 \times 10^{-8}$ Hills EMRIs per year per galaxy, approximately $12$ per cent of the classical EMRI yield. The Hills channel contribution to the overall eccentricity distribution depends strongly on the binary mass fraction in NSCs. We discuss implications for identifying EMRI formation channels with LISA and for quasi-periodic eruptions.

### [2] [Perpendicular transport in isotropic magnetic turbulence. The role of stochastic drifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13865)
*F. Pucci,P. Blasi,O. Pezzi,C. Evoli,F. Malara*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 结合测试粒子模拟与非线性导向中心理论，研究湍流磁场中垂直扩散的能量标度，发现高能下垂直扩散出现更强的能量依赖，源于随机漂移。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 宇宙线在湍流磁场中传播时，垂直扩散在多种天体环境中很重要。模拟显示平行与垂直扩散系数随能量标度不同，导致比值随能量增大，但缺乏完整理论解释。

Method: 采用各向同性合成湍流中的测试粒子模拟（波数跨越6-7个数量级），并扩展非线性导向中心理论以纳入随机漂移，将场线随机行走与随机导向中心漂移结合描述垂直输运。

Result: 仅当 r_L ≲ 10^{-2} L_c（r_L为拉莫尔半径，L_c为湍流相干长度）时，平行与垂直扩散系数具有相同能量标度；更高能量下垂直扩散呈现更强的能量依赖，且在不同湍流强度下一致。

Conclusion: 垂直扩散的更强能量依赖源于平行于平均磁场的湍流磁场分量所诱导的随机漂移，该结果提供了理论解释。

Abstract: In cosmic ray propagation through turbulent magnetic fields, transport perpendicular to the mean field plays an important role in various astrophysical environments. Numerical simulations have shown that parallel and perpendicular diffusion coefficients exhibit different energy scalings, causing their ratio to increase with energy, but a comprehensive theoretical explanation remains elusive. Here, we investigate perpendicular diffusion using test particle simulations in synthetic isotropic turbulence spanning 6-7 orders of magnitude in wavenumber, together with a theoretical extension of nonlinear guiding center theory that incorporates stochastic drifts. Our approach combines field line random walk and random guiding center drifts to describe perpendicular transport. We find that parallel and perpendicular diffusion coefficients exhibit the same energy scaling only for $r_L \lesssim 10^{-2} L_c$, where $r_L$ is the particle Larmor radius and $L_c$ is the turbulence coherence length. At higher energies, perpendicular diffusion develops a stronger energy dependence, consistently across different turbulence levels. Our theoretical model suggests that this behavior arises from stochastic drifts induced by turbulent magnetic field components parallel to the mean field.

### [3] [Adiabatic evolution of hadronic neutron stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13926)
*Domenico Logoteta,Francesco Zeno Constanzo,Ignazio Bombaci,Sebastiano Bernuzzi,Albino Perego,David Radice*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文利用有限温度β稳定状态方程计算静态与旋转中子星（含刚性/较差自转）的结构性质，引入超子与中微子囚禁，用于刻画原中子星及双中子星并合残余的长期演化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为理解核心坍缩超新星形成的原中子星（PNS）或双中子星并合（BNSM）残余的长期演化，需要包含超子与中微子囚禁的有限温度状态方程，并弥补动力学模拟无法覆盖完整演化时标的不足。

Method: 在Hartree近似下采用相对论平均场量子多体理论构建EOS，扩展Fortin等（2017）零温度强子EOS；耦合常数由超核与超核物质实验数据确定以降低超子单粒子势不确定性；考虑刚性自转与较差自转计算结构性质。

Result: EOS模型再现了冷中子星与PNS（核子及超子组分）的典型特征；与PSR J0348+0432（2.01 M_sun）和PSR J0740+6620（2.14 M_sun）的实测质量及GW170817的无量纲潮汐形变率一致；热效应对不同组分和自转状态的作用不同。

Conclusion: 该演化方案为解释BNSM残余或CCSNe PNS的长期演化提供了简单途径，可补充无法覆盖完整演化时标的动力学模拟。

Abstract: In this paper we compute several structural properties of static and rotating neutron stars, considering both rigid and differential rotation. To this end, we use a finite-temperature beta-stable equation of state (EOS) extending the zero-temperature hadronic EOS derived by Fortin et al. (2017). We account for the formation of hyperons and the presence of a neutrino-trapped component. Our goal is to provide insights into the long-term evolution of a protoneutron star (PNS) formed after a core-collapse supernova (CCSN) or a remnant resulting from a binary neutron star merger (BNSM). The EOS is derived using a relativistic mean-field quantum many-body theory within the Hartree approximation. Coupling constants are determined using experimental data on hypernuclei and hypernuclear matter to better account for uncertainties in hyperon single-particle potentials. Our EOS models reproduce the typical features of cold neutron stars and PNSs for nucleonic and hyperonic compositions. In particular, they are consistent with measured masses (2.01 M_sun and 2.14 M_sun for PSR J0348+0432 and PSR J0740+6620) and dimensionless tidal deformabilities from GW170817. Thermal contributions act differently depending on composition and rotation state. Finally, our evolutionary scheme provides a simple way to interpret the long-term evolution of BNSM remnants or PNSs from CCSNe, complementing dynamical simulations unable to cover full evolutionary timescales.

</details>
