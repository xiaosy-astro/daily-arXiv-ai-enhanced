### [1] [Are jet speeds governed by accretion modes?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02550)
*Hong Tu,Xinwu Cao,Andrzej A. Zdziarski*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文通过编译333个AGN射电喷流速度样本，发现类星体/FRII星系的喷流洛伦兹因子（均值11.5）显著高于BL Lac/FR I（均值4.2），后者与恒星质量黑洞X射线双星（BHXRB）相近，表明喷流速度与吸积模式强相关。作者提出磁驱动喷流的洛伦兹因子由喷流底部磁场压力与静止质量能量密度之比决定，盘主导磁场（类星体）比ADAF主导磁场（BL Lac）更强，从而解释了不同类型源的喷流速度系统性差异。

Motivation: 尽管相对论性喷流同时存在于恒星质量黑洞X射线双星（BHXRBs）和活动星系核（AGNs）中，但两者的体洛伦兹因子存在系统性差异（BHXRB通常<2，而AGN可高达~50），这一差异的起源尚不清楚。作者试图通过大样本统计和模型计算来揭示喷流速度差异与吸积模式之间的关系。

Method: 作者从文献中编译了333个具有良好喷流分量运动测量的AGN样本（270个类星体、47个BL Lac、10个FR I、6个FR II），测量其体洛伦兹因子。随后基于磁驱动喷流理论，提出洛伦兹因子主要由喷流基部的磁压与静止质量能量密度之比决定，并分别对ADAF维持磁场（BL Lac/FR I/BHXRB）和吸积盘维持磁场（类星体/FR II）两种情形进行模型计算比较。

Result: 类星体/FR II的洛伦兹因子范围为~1.0-41.5，均值11.5（中位数9.5）；BL Lac/FR I的范围为~1.0-21.9，均值4.2（中位数1.5）。BL Lac/FR I的值（尤其是中位数）与BHXRBs非常接近。模型计算表明吸积盘产生的磁场始终强于ADAF的磁场，因而能产生更大的喷流洛伦兹因子。

Conclusion: 喷流速度与吸积模式之间存在强相关关系：在ADAF吸积模式下（BL Lac/FR I/BHXRB），喷流洛伦兹因子较低；而在标准薄盘吸积模式下（类星体/FR II），洛伦兹因子较高。这种差异源于两种吸积模式下维持喷流磁场的强度不同，为理解不同尺度黑洞系统中喷流加速机制的系统性差异提供了统一的理论解释。

Abstract: Relativistic jets are observed in both stellar-mass black hole X-ray binaries (BHXRBs) and active galactic nuclei (AGNs), yet their bulk Lorentz factors differ systematically---those in black hole X-ray binaries are typically below $\sim2$, whereas AGN jets can reach $\sim50$. The origin of this discrepancy remains unclear. Searching the literature, we compile a sample of 333 AGNs with well-measured jet component motions, consisting of 270 quasars, 47 BL Lac objects, 10 FR I, and 6 FR II galaxies. We find that quasars/FR\,IIs exhibit minimal bulk Lorentz factors ranging from $\sim$1.0 to 41.5, with a mean of 11.5 (median 9.5). In contrast, BL Lac objects/FR\,Is show $Γ_{\rm jet}\sim$1.0--21.9, averaging 4.2 (median 1.5). These values, particularly the median, closely resemble those of BHXRBs, implying a strong correlation between jet speed and accretion mode. The Lorentz factor of a magnetically driven jet is mainly determined by the ratio of the magnetic pressure to rest mass energy density at the jet base. In BL Lacs/FRIs/BHXRBs, the field is maintained by the advection-dominated accretion flow (ADAF), and the gas at the ADAF surface is magnetically driven into the jets. In quasars/FRIIs, the field is maintained by the disc, while the jet base is connected to the corona. Our model calculations show that the disc field is always much stronger than that of the ADAF, and therefore leads to a larger $Γ_{\rm jet}$, which can explain the systematic difference in $Γ_{\rm jet}$ between these two types of sources.
