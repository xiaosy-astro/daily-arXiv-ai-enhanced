### [1] [Peering through the dip: IXPE unveils the extended scattering environment of GX 13+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28714)
*Alessandro Di Marco,Fabio La Monaca,Luigi Stella,Anagha P. Nitindala,Alexandra Veledina,Anna Bobrikova,Eleonora Veronica Lai,Alessandro Papitto,Maura Pilia,Juri Poutanen,Daniele Rogantini,Matteo Bachetti,Maria Cristina Baglio,Caterina Ballocco,Ettore Del Monte,Wei Deng,Giulia Illiano,Vladislav Loktev,Sara Motta,Fei Xie*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文首次利用IXPE、NuSTAR和Swift-XRT对高倾角挖食源GX 13+1进行协调观测，监测其周期性挖食事件。研究发现挖食中心处偏振度达到约9%（>8σ置信度），且从挖食到非挖食状态偏振角旋转约60°，并表现出能量依赖的偏振特性。通过偏振建模，约束了散射介质的几何结构，为吸积盘冕或盘风的延伸散射环境提供了关键几何约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 中子星低质量X射线双星（LMXBs）具有复杂的吸积几何结构，常包含吸积盘冕或盘风。理解这些散射介质的几何结构对于揭示吸积过程至关重要，但传统光谱和时变分析难以直接约束其空间几何。X射线偏振测量提供了一种独特的探测手段，然而此前尚无针对此类源周期性挖食事件的偏振观测。

Method: 采用IXPE、NuSTAR和Swift-XRT的协调多波段观测，对高度倾角的挖食源GX 13+1进行系统性监测。通过分析挖食期间的偏振度、偏振角及其随时间（挖食相位）和能量（能谱分辨）的变化，结合物理模型对偏振数据进行拟合，以约束散射介质的几何参数（如ADC的赤道/极半径比、盘风的电子密度和开角等）。

Result: 挖食中心偏振度达~9%（>8σ），偏振角在挖食到非挖食状态间旋转约60°，偏振具有能量依赖性。模型拟合表明：若为扁椭球延伸吸积盘冕（ADC），其赤道半径至少为极半径的1.5倍，光学深度τ~0.3；若为盘风散射，则最可能电子密度为1.3×10^14 cm^-3，开角约40°，对应光学深度~0.2。

Conclusion: 该研究首次展示了X射线偏振数据在揭示吸积盘周围延伸散射环境几何结构方面的关键作用，显著推进了对中子星低质量X射线双星系统的理解，确立了偏振测量作为诊断此类天体吸积几何的重要工具。

Abstract: Neutron star low-mass X-ray binaries feature complex accretion geometries, often including an accretion disk corona or disk winds. Here, a study of the highly inclined dipping source GX 13+1, using coordinated observations from the IXPE, NuSTAR, and Swift-XRT, is presented; this is the first time that such a campaign was conducted to monitor its periodic dip. Our analysis reveals significant variations in polarimetric properties tracking the dip. At the center of the dip, the polarization degree reaches a maximum of ${\sim}$9% (at more than $8σ$ confidence level). This is accompanied by a highly significant polarization angle rotation of roughly 60$^\circ$ when passing from the dip to the subsequent off-dip state. Furthermore, the dip state exhibits energy-dependent polarization. By modeling the polarization during the periodic dip, we constrain the geometry of the scattering medium. In the scenario of an oblate extended accretion disk corona (ADC), an equatorial radius at least 1.5 times its polar radius is needed with $τ{\sim}0.3$. Alternatively, the results can be modeled as scattering in a disk wind with a most probable electron density of $1.3\times10^{14}$ cm$^{-3}$ and an opening angle near 40$^\circ$, corresponding to an optical depth of ${\sim}0.2$. The obtained results highlight the crucial role of X-ray polarimetric data to unveil the geometry of the extended scattering environment surrounding the accretion disks, advancing our understanding of neutron star low-mass X-ray binaries.

### [2] [Rates of tidal disruption events from constrained cosmological simulations of the local Universe: population properties and implications for transient surveys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29454)
*Julian S. Sommer,Ildar Khabibullin,Klaus Dolag,Luca Sala,Benjamin Seidel,Jenny G. Sorce,Alice Damiano*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文利用约束宇宙学模拟SLOW，在完全三维框架下估算潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）速率，覆盖Coma、Hercules、Shapley、Virgo、Perseus超星系团及Fornax星系团六个环境。研究发现平均体积TDE速率约为600 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹，每个黑洞约4.5×10⁻⁵ yr⁻¹。与早期文献绝对速率一致，但空间分布根本不同：核心区速率因动力学消耗和直接捕获约束而大幅降低，总TDE预算由延展星系团晕中的尖核卫星星系主导。TDE产出由黑洞人口结构和空间聚集度驱动而非总质量，活跃组装的超星系团降低每黑洞效率，而低质量环境效率更高。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 即将到来的巡天项目（如LSST）将观测到大量TDE事件，但传统二维解析外推法在预测TDE速率方面存在局限。作者希望利用完全三维的约束宇宙学模拟，在更真实的宇宙环境中提供可靠的TDE预算估计，以弥补观测预测与理论模拟之间的差距。

Method: 使用约束宇宙学模拟SLOW，对六个环境（Coma、Hercules、Shapley、Virgo、Perseus超星系团和Fornax星系团）的zoom-in边界体（径向范围55-92 Mpc）提取黑洞人口统计（含自旋）及宿主星系性质。建立过滤方案以严格保留动力学稳定的"主序"黑洞，根据1 kpc内三维恒星密度剖面斜率将宿主星系分类为尖核或核球状，并对过滤样本应用相对论Kesden效率修正。

Result: 六个环境的平均体积TDE速率约为600 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹，每黑洞速率约为4.5×10⁻⁵ yr⁻¹。绝对速率与早期文献一致，但空间分布根本不同：核心区速率因动力学消耗和直接捕获约束大幅降低，TDE预算由延展星系团晕中的尖核卫星星系主导。TDE产出由黑洞人口结构和空间集中度驱动而非星系团总质量。活跃组装的超星系团（如Hercules）因并合驱动的黑洞质量增长降低每黑洞TDE效率，而低质量环境（如Fornax）因未并合的低质量黑洞而效率更高。

Conclusion: 传统二维解析方法低估了TDE空间分布的复杂性。TDE速率主要受黑洞人口特征（质量、密度、空间分布）驱动，而非简单依赖宿主环境的总质量。这对LSST等巡天项目的TDE预测具有重要启示：应关注尖核卫星星系和延展晕区域的贡献，同时考虑不同环境组装历史对TDE效率的差异性影响。

Abstract: (abridged) Motivated by upcoming surveys like LSST, we estimate tidal disruption event (TDE) rates using the constrained cosmological Simulation of the LOcal Web (SLOW) to test the limitations of traditional 2D analytical extrapolations within a fully 3D framework. We aim to provide reliable TDE budgets extracted from the simulated zoom-in volumes of the digital counterparts of the Coma, Hercules, Shapley, Virgo, and Perseus supercluster environments, and the Fornax galaxy cluster. From the zoom-in boundary volumes of the six environments, reaching radial extents of $55-92\,$Mpc, we extracted black hole demographics (including spin) and their host galaxy properties to establish a filter scheme that strictly preserves dynamically stable "main-sequence" black holes. We further classified host galaxies as cuspy or cored based on the slope of their 3D stellar density profile measured within $1\,$kpc as a proxy for unresolved nuclear structure and applied the relativistic Kesden efficiency correction to the filtered sample. We find an average volumetric and per black hole TDE rate of $\approx 600\,$Gpc$^{-3}\,$yr$^{-1}$ and $\approx 4.5\times 10^{-5}\,$yr$^{-1}$ across all six environments, respectively. Although our absolute TDE rates match early literature estimates, the underlying spatial distribution fundamentally differs. Central core rates are heavily reduced by dynamical depletion and direct capture constraints, meaning the total TDE budget is overwhelmingly dominated by cuspy satellite galaxies in the extended cluster halos. TDE yields are driven by black hole demographics and spatial concentration rather than total cluster mass. Actively assembling superclusters (e.g., Hercules) reduce per-black-hole TDE efficiencies via merger-driven black hole mass growth, whereas low-mass environments (e.g., Fornax) are highly efficient due to unmerged, low-mass black holes.

### [3] [Gamma-ray emission from particle illumination and shock-cloud interaction in the W51 Complex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29488)
*Alan Sunny,Martina Cardillo*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文研究了W51复合体在超高能伽马射线波段的两个强子模型。直接激波-云相互作用模型能解释Fermi-LAT观测的GeV辐射，但无法解释LHAASO探测的UHE发射；而"照明"（illumination）模型（云作为长期粒子约束区域）则能自然解释UHE谱。研究指出致密分子云是维持历史PeVatron活动和塑造观测到的UHE伽马射线发射的关键结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在甚高能(UHE)和超高能(VHE)伽马射线天文时代，理解银河系PeVatron及其加速机制是首要目标。LHAASO对W51复合体的观测使其成为研究UHE辐射起源的理想实验室，因为该区域存在大量致密分子环境，且周围有多个潜在的粒子加速器。作者旨在厘清UHE发射的起源机制。

Method: 作者构建了两种强子模型：(1) 直接激波-云相互作用模型：模拟W51C超新星遗迹与W51B中邻近分子云的直接作用，包含新鲜粒子加速、激波驱动的绝热压缩以及银河宇宙线的再加速过程；(2) 与加速器无关的照明模型：假设W51B分子云作为高能粒子的长期约束区域，这些粒子在更早时期被注入并持续存在于云中。

Result: 直接激波-云相互作用模型能够成功再现Fermi-LAT观测到的GeV发射，但无法解释LHAASO检测到的UHE辐射。相比之下，照明模型能够自然解释UHE谱，表明致密分子云可以在长时间尺度内有效约束和维持高能强子粒子群。

Conclusion: 尽管推断的注入历史与年轻超新星遗迹起源相容，但照明框架与源无关的性质也允许W51复合体内其他加速器作为粒子来源。研究结论认为致密分子环境是维持历史PeVatron活动并塑造观测到的UHE伽马射线发射的关键结构。

Abstract: In the current era of very-high-energy (VHE) and ultra-high-energy (UHE) $γ$-ray astronomy, understanding Galactic PeVatrons and their acceleration mechanisms remains a primary objective. Recent LHAASO observations of the W51 Complex make it an ideal laboratory for investigating the origin of UHE emission, particularly due to the presence of massive and dense molecular environment surrounding multiple potential particle accelerators. In this work, we study two hadronic scenarios for the W51 Complex. First, we model the direct interaction between the SNR W51C and the nearby clouds in W51B, incorporating fresh particle acceleration, shock-driven adiabatic compression, and reacceleration of permeating Galactic cosmic rays. Second, we explore an accelerator-independent illumination scenario in which the W51B cloud acts as a long-term confinement region for high-energy particles injected during an earlier epoch. We find that the direct shock-cloud interaction scenario successfully reproduces the GeV emission observed by Fermi-LAT, but fails to account for the UHE emission detected by LHAASO. In contrast, the illumination scenario naturally explains the UHE spectrum, indicating that dense molecular clouds can efficiently confine and sustain energetic hadronic populations over long timescales. Although the inferred injection history is compatible with a young SNR origin, the source-independent nature of the illumination framework also permits other accelerators within the W51 Complex. Our results therefore identify dense molecular environments as the key structures sustaining historical PeVatron activity and shaping the observed UHE $γ$-ray emission.
