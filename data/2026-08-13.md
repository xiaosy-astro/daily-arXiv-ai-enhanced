### [1] [ATLAS22kjn (AT 2022fpx): A Coronal Line Emitter with an Early Light Curve Bump and Mid-Infrared Dust Echo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11300)
*Athena C. Engholm,Jason T. Hinkle,Benjamin J. Shappee,Katie Auchettl,Dhvanil D. Desai,Willem B. Hoogendam,Christopher S. Kochanek,Nicholas Earl,K. Decker French,Michael A. Tucker,Chris Ashall,Aaron Do,Allison Blum,Thomas de Jaeger,Mark E. Huber,Anna Payne,Jose L. Prieto*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文分析了潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）ATLAS22kjn（AT 2022fpx），该事件具有高电离日冕线（CLs）和峰值前的光变曲线凸起等独特特征。研究发现其日冕线在UV/光学峰值前出现、比X射线早约300天，并持续到X射线消失后，表明X射线在早期和晚期均被遮挡。CL光度显示电离源温度在500天内下降不到10%。UV/光学光变曲线中观测到峰值前125天出现、持续9天的凸起，与流-流碰撞或风-流碰撞的前驱特征理论预测一致。该事件还显示出显著的尘埃回波（MIR），尘埃覆盖因子fc≈0.40±0.03。研究估算出核区各发射区域的相对大小，确定CL区位于宽线区和MIR尘埃之间。

Motivation: 潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）是高能天体物理中的重要现象，但其极端紫外（EUV）/超软X射线辐射通常难以直接观测。ATLAS22kjn作为一个同时具有高电离日冕线和峰值前光变曲线凸起的特殊TDE，为揭示TDE的物理机制、探测被遮挡的EUV/X射线辐射以及理解核区发射区域结构提供了独特机会。

Method: 本研究通过对ATLAS22kjn进行多波段观测分析，包括：1) 利用光学光谱中日冕线（CLs）的光度约束电离辐射源的温度演化；2) 分析UV/光学光变曲线，识别并表征峰值前的凸起特征（约9天持续时间，峰值前125天出现）；3) 通过中红外（MIR）光变曲线分析尘埃回波，估算尘埃覆盖因子；4) 整合多波段观测数据，估算核区各发射区域的相对半径和大小。

Result: 研究发现：日冕线比X射线早约300天出现，并在X射线消失后持续存在，表明X射线在早期和晚期均被遮挡；电离源温度在500天内下降不到10%；光变曲线前驱凸起的时标和光度与流-流碰撞或风-流碰撞的理论预测最一致；MIR尘埃回波显示高尘埃覆盖因子fc≈0.40±0.03，与其他CL发射TDE相似；CL发射区位于宽线区和MIR尘埃之间。

Conclusion: ATLAS22kjn展示了多波段和早期观测的重要性，并证实了日冕线（CLEs）在表征TDE中其他方式无法观测的EUV/超软X射线辐射方面的有效性。该研究为理解TDE的核区几何结构、辐射遮蔽机制以及前驱光变特征（可能源于流-流碰撞）提供了重要约束。

Abstract: We present an analysis of ATLAS22kjn (AT 2022fpx), whose high-ionisation coronal lines (CLs) and pre-peak light curve bump provide distinctive opportunities to investigate the physical mechanisms powering tidal disruption events (TDEs). In addition to CLs, the optical spectra show common TDE features, including a strong, blue continuum and broad Balmer and He II lines. The CLs appear before UV/optical light curve peak, preceding the detection of X-rays by $\sim 300$ days and persisting after X-rays are no longer detected, suggesting the X-ray emission is obscured at both early and late times. Using the CL luminosities, we constrain the temperature evolution of the ionising source, finding a decrease of $\lesssim 10 \%$ over 500 days. In the UV/optical light curve, we observe a $9 \substack{+4 \\ -2}$ day bump that peaks $125 \substack{+5 \\ -3}$ rest-frame days before the peak of the main flare. Although we cannot definitively determine the origins of the bump, we find that its timescale and luminosity are most consistent with theoretical predictions for a precursor feature produced by a stream-stream collision or a wind-stream collision. ATLAS22kjn also shows a prominent dust echo in its mid-infrared (MIR) light curves, indicating a high dust covering fraction $f_c \simeq 0.40 \pm 0.03$, similar to the covering fractions of other CL-emitting TDEs. From the multi-wavelength observations of ATLAS22kjn, we estimate the size and relative radii of the emission regions in its nuclear environment and determine that the CL region lies between the broad line region and the MIR-emitting dust. ATLAS22kjn demonstrates the importance of multi-wavelength and early-time observations, and the utility of CLEs in characterising the otherwise unobservable EUV/ultrasoft X-ray emission of TDEs.
