### [1] [Impact of eccentricity and higher-modes on neutron star-black hole parameter estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16135)
*Snehal Tibrewal,Aasim Jan,Aaron Zimmerman,Hsin-Yu Chen*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文系统研究了偏心轨道对中子星-黑洞并合引力波信号参数估计的影响，发现偏心率的增加能显著提升对偏心率、有效自旋和质量比等参数的测量精度，但对光度距离和天区定位等外部参数改善有限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 中子星-黑洞并合系统能够提供极端物质状态、双星形成通道以及致密天体性质等重要信息，而信号中的偏心特征可以进一步提升参数估计精度并揭示双星形成机制。由于以往对偏心率的测量较弱，本文旨在系统评估偏心对参数推断的影响。

Method: 采用最先进的偏心波形模型SEOBNRv5EHM进行注入模拟，以GW200105_162426为参考系统，系统性地开展参数估计研究，比较不同偏心率下各参数的测量精度。

Result: 偏心率越高，对偏心率本身的测量精度越高（e=0.25时1σ不确定度低至4×10^{-4}）；有效自旋和质量比的约束分别比准圆形情况改善约13倍和20倍；但外部参数（光度距离、天区定位）没有显著改善。

Conclusion: 偏心特征对中子星-黑洞系统的内部参数（偏心率、有效自旋、质量比）估计有显著提升作用，但对外部参数估计帮助有限，表明偏心提供的信息与高阶模式提供的信息可能存在简并或重叠。

Abstract: Detections of gravitational waves from neutron star-black hole systems provide avenues for studying extreme matter, constraining binary formation channels, and testing the nature of compact objects in strong gravity. Eccentric signatures in the signal further enhance this potential by improving parameter estimation and offering clues about binary formation. Because eccentricity is primarily imprinted during the inspiral phase, it is often weakly constrained or missed entirely in binary black hole observations; in contrast, neutron star-black hole systems produce longer in-band signals, enabling more precise measurements of eccentricity and leaving a distinct imprint on parameter inference. In this work, we present a systematic parameter-estimation study exploring the impact of eccentricity on inference using injections simulated with the state-of-the-art eccentric waveform model SEOBNRv5EHM. We find that for systems like GW200105_162426, the measurement precision of eccentricity and correlated parameters improves as eccentricity increases, yielding tighter constraints at larger eccentricities. For the highest eccentricity considered in this study, $e=0.25$, we recover eccentricity with $1σ$ uncertainty as low as $4\times10^{-4}$. In addition, the constraints on effective spin $χ_\mathrm{eff}$ and mass ratio $q$ improve relative to the quasi-circular case by factors of $\sim13$ and $\sim20$, respectively. On the other hand, we find no significant improvement in extrinsic parameters such as luminosity distance and sky localization, suggesting that for systems like GW200105_162426, the additional information provided by eccentricity in this sector is either negligible or degenerate with the information provided by higher-order modes.

### [2] [Eccentricity as a Magnifying Glass: Precision Population Inference Enabled by Eccentric Neutron Star--Black Hole Mergers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16136)
*Alberto Salvarese,Hsin-Yu Chen,Aaron Zimmerman,Snehal Tibrewal*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该论文研究了偏心轨道如何提升中子星-黑洞（NSBH）合并系统的参数测量精度，进而改善对其形成通道的推断能力。研究发现偏心系统能更精确地测量有效自旋参数、中子星质量等，有助于区分不同形成通道，并在O5观测运行时即可检验动力学形成通道的种群级偏心分布。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 致密双星系统的形成历史是天体物理学中尚未解决的关键问题之一。现有理论通常认为NSBH系统主要通过孤立双星演化形成（近圆轨道），但GW200105事件的可测量偏心率暗示存在其他形成通道，因此需要研究如何利用偏心系统来更好地区分不同形成通道。

Method: 通过比较偏心NSBH合并与圆轨道NSBH合并的内在参数（如质量、自旋）测量精度差异，分析偏心增强的参数测量如何影响形成通道的推断。利用LVK观测数据，评估有效自旋参数χ_eff和中子星质量的测量改进，以及这些改进对种群统计推断的影响。

Result: 偏心系统可更精确测量组件质量和自旋。有效自旋参数χ_eff的锐化测量使得在O5观测运行中，每~2.5个偏心NSBH探测中就能明确识别一个自旋-轨道未对准事件。改进的NS质量测量能更好地约束NS质量分布的上限和下限，并揭示其结构。同时，依赖金属丰度的BH质量分布的恢复也得到改善。

Conclusion: 偏心增强的参数测量能够显著改善NSBH形成通道的推断能力，并且仅在O5观测运行结束时即可对动力学形成通道所预测的种群级偏心分布进行检验。

Abstract: The formation history of compact binary systems remains one of the key open questions in astrophysics. Theoretical studies generally favor isolated binary evolution for neutron star-black hole (NSBH) systems, which tends to produce nearly circular orbits. However, recent analyses of the gravitational-wave event GW200105 indicate that its source has measurable eccentricity, suggesting that alternative formation channels may also contribute. It has been shown that the intrinsic parameters of eccentric NSBH mergers, such as the component masses and spins, are much better measured than circular mergers with LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (LVK) observatories. We explore how such eccentricity-enhanced parameter measurements can affect the inference of NSBH formation channels. We find that sharper measurements of the effective spin parameter $χ_{\rm eff}$ increase the fraction of systems for which negative values can be confidently identified, allowing for the clear measurement of a spin-orbit misaligned event every $\sim 2.5$ eccentric NSBH detections for an isotropically distributed population in the fifth LVK observing run (O5). Improved NS mass measurements provide better constraining power for NS mass distributions, potentially revealing structure and tightening the bounds that can be drawn on their upper and lower masses. Similarly, the recovery of a metallicity-dependent BH mass distribution is improved by eccentricity-enhanced measurements. Finally, we show that the proposed population-level eccentricity distribution for dynamical-formation channels can be tested by the end of O5.

### [3] [X-ray polarization of Z-type neutron star low-mass X-ray binaries -- II. Spectropolarimetric analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16140)
*Andrea Gnarini,Francesco Ursini,Giorgio Matt,Stefano Bianchi,Fiamma Capitanio,Massimo Cocchi,Sergio Fabiani,Ruben Farinelli,Philip Kaaret,Lorenzo Marra,Antonella Tarana*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文利用IXPE、NICER和NuSTAR数据，对Z型中子星低质量X射线双星（NS-LMXBs）在不同分支（水平分支、正常分支、耀发分支）上的X射线偏振进行了详细的光谱偏振分析，发现康普顿化是X射线发射和偏振的主要贡献者，偏振度在水平分支最高（约6%），正常分支降至3-4%，且观测到的偏振度显著高于理论预期，盘偏振角度与康普顿化偏振角度存在明显错位。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Z型中子星低质量X射线双星在其颜色-颜色图（CCD）上的不同分支展现出独特的X射线偏振特征，先前研究发现其水平分支可达目前观测到的NS-LMXBs最高偏振度（2-8 keV）。然而，对这些源在不同分支上的详细光谱偏振特性及其物理机制的理解仍不充分，且观测到的偏振度与理论预期存在显著差异，需要更深入的分析。

Method: 对多个Z型源（GX 5-1除外）在不同分支上的数据，结合IXPE、NICER和NuSTAR的观测，使用热吸积盘发射加较硬康普顿化成分的连续谱模型，并考虑相对论展宽铁线等反射特征，进行详细的光谱偏振分析，提取各分支的偏振度（PD）和偏振角（PA）。

Result: （1）康普顿化是X射线发射和偏振的主要贡献者；（2）水平分支偏振度约6%，正常分支降至3-4%，耀发分支由于曝光时间短约束较弱；（3）观测到的偏振度显著高于典型的扩展层或边界层理论预期；（4）盘的偏振度低于3%，但仍高于电子散射主导的平行平面大气模型预测；（5）盘的偏振角与康普顿化偏振角明显不垂直（错位）；（6）偏振信号与倾角或反射光子贡献无相关性。

Conclusion: Z型NS-LMXBs的X射线偏振主要由康普顿化主导，其偏振度远超现有理论模型预测，表明当前对吸积边界层/扩展层的几何结构理解需要修正；盘偏振角度与康普顿化角度的错位暗示两者几何结构并非简单垂直关系。

Abstract: IXPE has provided for the first time detailed energy- and time-resolved X-ray polarimetry of Z-type neutron star low-mass X-ray binaries (NS-LMXBs) as they move along their color-color diagrams (CCDs). These sources can reach the highest polarization observed for NS-LXMBs in the 2-8 keV range when they move along the horizontal branch. In a previous paper, we characterized the spectral state of a sample of Z-sources using the CCD and estimated the polarization with model-independent analysis. Here, we present detailed spectropolarimetric analysis for each source on each branch using data from IXPE, NICER, and NuSTAR. The continuum X-ray emission of all the sources is well described with a combination of thermal accretion disk emission plus a harder Comptonized component. In addition, reflection features, in particular the relativistically broadened Fe line, are observed for our sources, except GX 5-1. For most of the sources and branches, the main contribution to the X-ray emission and polarization is due to Comptonization: moving from the horizontal branch (HB) to the normal branch (NB), the polarization degree (PD) in the 2-8 keV band varies from about 6% to 3-4%, while the PD is loosely constrained in the flaring branch (FB), due to the shorter exposures. These PD values are significantly higher than theoretical expectations for typical spreading or boundary layer configurations. The polarization of the disk is generally lower (below 3%) but still higher than predictions for an electron scattering-dominated, plane-parallel atmosphere above the disk observed at the corresponding inclination. Moreover, the polarization angle (PA) of the disk seems to be significantly misaligned and not perpendicular to that of Comptonization. We find no correlation between the polarization signal and the inclination, nor with the contribution of reflected photons throughout the Z-track.

</details>
