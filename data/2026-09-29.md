### [1] [Deciphering the VHE gamma-rays spectra of Extreme High-Frequency peaked BL Lac Objects through a Photohadronic Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30641)
*Sarira Sahu,D. I. Páez-Sánchez,A. U. Puga Oliveros,M. E. Iglesias Martínez,Jose Guerra Carmenate,R. de J. Pacheco-Aké,G. Sánchez-Colón,Subhash Rajpoot,P. Fernández de Córdoba,Gaetano Lambiase*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 用光强子模型结合EBL修正分析15个EHBL源的22个VHE能谱，模型能较好拟合观测，并按光子谱指数δ分类，还约束了HESS J1943+213的红移。

Motivation: EHBL因低光度常难被探测，已知数量少，理解不足，因此需要对其VHE能谱进行系统建模与分析。

Method: 采用光强子（photohadronic）模型并加入河外背景光（EBL）修正，分析不同时期观测的15个EHBL源的22个VHE伽马射线谱。

Result: 该光强子模型能合理描述观测到的VHE能谱分布；据光子谱指数δ对源分类；约束了HESS J1943+213的红移；多数耀发期处于高发射态，其次为低发射态和极高发射态。

Conclusion: 所研究的耀发期比例仅代表当前样本而非整个EHBL群体；H 2356-309的异常VHE谱暗示其可能是一类具有硬谱和极软内禀谱的新EHBL子类。

Abstract: Extreme high-frequency peaked BL Lac objects (EHBLs) have their synchrotron peak above $\sim 1$ keV and their inverse Compton peak extends up to several TeVs. Due to low luminosity of EHBLs, these objects are often undetected, and only a small number of them are thus known. As a result, they are poorly understood. Using a photohadronic model and including extragalactic background light correction we analyzed 22 very high-energy (VHE) gamma-ray spectra of 15 EHBL sources observed in different periods. We find that the photohadronic model used can adequately describe the observed VHE spectral energy distribution and furthermore, we classify them according to the value of the photon spectral index, $δ$. The redshift of the EHBL HESS J1943+213 is also tightly constrained using the same photohadronic model. Within the sample, a plurality of flaring epochs fall in the high-emission state which is followed by the low-emission state and the very-high-emission state respectively. These fractions of flaring epochs characterize only the present sample, not the EHBL population at large. Due to the anomalous behavior of the VHE gamma-ray spectra of the EHBL H 2356-309, we suggest it to be a new subclass of EHBL having hard and very soft intrinsic spectra during different flaring epochs.
