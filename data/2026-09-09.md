### [1] [Electromagnetic alignment and jet precession around supermassive black holes: Quasi-periodic oscillations in tidal disruption events](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05350)
*Pau Amaro-Seoane,Leif Lui,Alejandro Torres-Orjuela,Xian Chen*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文利用协变电磁角动量转移框架，研究了潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）中的准周期振荡（QPO）与喷流形成机制。广义相对论框架拖曳撕裂错位的瞬态吸积流形成内迷你盘，磁场在该盘上的锚定使喷流轴旋转，产生X射线和射电通量的几何调制。通过小自旋展开和分裂单极磁场拓扑，推导出电磁反作用力矩的解析形式，表明该力矩驱动迷你盘逆行进动，与顺行的Lense-Thirring进动耦合决定全局振荡频率，并给出了可观测信号的定量预测。

Motivation: 理解TDE中QPO和喷流形成的物理机制，并建立从可观测信号中提取黑洞自旋和磁通密度的独立方法。

Method: 使用Blandford-Znajek机制的协变电磁角动量转移公式，采用分裂单极磁场拓扑建模电磁反作用，通过事件视界上Noether电流密度的小自旋展开推导解析反作用力矩，分析迷你盘的前进动耦合动力学。

Result: 电磁反作用诱发迷你盘逆行进动，与顺行Lense-Thirring进动耦合决定全局振荡频率；预测了通量比的单调衰减、频率漂移（先延长后缩短的时间间隔）以及晚期恒定残余调制幅度等可观测特征。

Conclusion: 建立了TDE中QPO与喷流进动的解析机制，提供了一种独立于谱连续拟合的黑洞自旋与磁通密度提取方法。

Abstract: We evaluate quasi-periodic oscillations and jet formation in tidal disruption events using the covariant formulation of electromagnetic angular-momentum transfer. General-relativistic frame-dragging tears apart misaligned transient accretion flows, forming an isolated inner mini-disk. The accumulation of magnetic flux on the event horizon powers a relativistic jet via the Blandford-Znajek mechanism. Because the magnetic field anchors to the precessing mini-disk, the jet axis rotates, generating geometric modulations in the observed X-ray and radio fluxes. To ensure physical consistency with the force-free magnetosphere required to launch a Blandford-Znajek jet, we model the electromagnetic back-reaction using a split-monopole magnetic field topology. By performing a small-spin expansion of the Noether current density over the event horizon, we derive a closed-form analytical reaction torque exerted by the electromagnetic field on the accretion plasma. We evaluate the resulting kinematics to show that the electromagnetic back-reaction induces a retrograde precession of the mini-disk, coupling with the prograde Lense-Thirring precession to dictate the global oscillation frequency. We formulate explicit predictions for observable transient signals and predict a monotonic attenuation of the peak-to-trough flux ratio as the mini-disk aligns, as well as a specific frequency drift signature characterized by an initial lengthening followed by an asymptotic shortening of the time interval between consecutive flares. We establish an analytical mechanism where magnetic flux depletion stalls alignment, predicting a constant residual modulation amplitude at late times. We formulate a methodology to extract the black hole spin and the magnetic flux density directly from the temporal derivatives of this predicted frequency drift, operating independently of spectral continuum fitting.
