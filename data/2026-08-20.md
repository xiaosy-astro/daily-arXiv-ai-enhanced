### [1] [Controlling artificial surface heating in neutron star simulations: Application to hybrid equations of state](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16945)
*Georgios Doulis*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文研究了熵基通量限制（EFL）方案在有限差分代码BAM中缓解双中子星模拟中恒星表面数值加热伪影的效果。通过对孤立中子星和双中子星旋近的模拟，使用混合状态方程，作者证明EFL方案能显著减少人工表面加热，且这一效果在所有恒星模型和双星构型中均保持一致，表明EFL方法减少数值加热具有鲁棒性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 双中子星模拟中恒星表面的处理对数值演化精度和可观测量的物理可靠性至关重要。接近恒星表面陡峭梯度处的数值伪影会在旋近过程中产生虚假加热，导致内能人为增加和中子星非物理膨胀，影响模拟结果的准确性。

Method: 作者在有限差分代码BAM中采用熵基通量限制（EFL）方案，对孤立中子星和双中子星旋近系统进行模拟，并使用一组具有代表性的混合状态方程来检验该方案的有效性。

Result: EFL方案显著减少了人工表面加热，且这种减少在所有考虑的恒星模型和双星构型中均一致地观察到，证明了EFL方法减少数值加热是一种稳健的特征。

Conclusion: EFL方案是缓解双中子星模拟中表面数值加热伪影的有效且稳健的方法，能够在不同物理条件下一致性地改善模拟精度和物理可靠性。

Abstract: The treatment of the stellar surface in binary neutron star simulations is crucial for the accuracy of the numerical evolution and the physical reliability of the predicted observables. Numerical artifacts associated with the treatment of steep gradients near the stellar surface can produce spurious heating during the inspiral, leading to an artificial increase of the internal energy and an unphysical expansion of the neutron star. In this work, we investigate the effectiveness of the entropy-based flux-limiting (EFL) scheme in mitigating these numerical effects within the finite-difference code BAM. We perform simulations of both isolated neutron stars and binary neutron star inspirals employing a representative set of hybrid equations of state. We show that the EFL scheme significantly reduces artificial surface heating. This reduction is observed consistently across all stellar models and binary configurations considered, demonstrating that the reduction of numerical heating is a robust feature of the EFL method.

### [2] [NMMA-Astro-COLIBRI: An Automated Light-Curve Supernovae Classification Service in the Multi-Survey Era](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17568)
*R. Weizmann Kiendrebeogo,Bernardo Cornejo Avila,Sofia Bisero,Maxime Cellier,Antoine Ciric,Ilja Jaroschewski,Henrik Rose,Alexandre Saint-Paul,Fabian Schüssler*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文介绍了NMMA-Astro-COLIBRI，一个将贝叶斯推断框架与实时多信使平台相结合的按需瞬变天体分类服务。该系统利用嵌套采样对光学瞬变的光度数据进行11种超新星模板拟合，并通过贝叶斯因子进行定量排名。在SN 2021ugl（Type IIb超新星）上验证了工作流，证明其能在光谱确认前10天正确分类，优于kilonova模板和激波冷却模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 宽视场巡天产生的大量实时光度警报需要自动化工具进行实时瞬变分类，特别是要区分超新星与kilonova候选体，以避免错误分类并提高多信使天文学的效率。

Method: 将NMMA贝叶斯推断框架耦合到Astro-COLIBRI实时平台。工作流程包括：检测到光学瞬变后过滤光度数据，使用嵌套采样对用户选择的11种超新星模板模型进行拟合，通过计算对数贝叶斯因子对不同模型进行定量排名，结果在几分钟内交付给用户。

Result: 在SN 2021ugl（Type IIb超新星）上验证：仅使用前6天两个波段（ZTF g和r）的光度数据（比光谱确认早10天），经验Type IIb模板即正确分类，优于kilonova模板和激波冷却模型。在完整的47天三波段数据中，同样获得最高证据值。

Conclusion: 全面的超新星模板库对于在多巡天时代的kilonova判别至关重要，NMMA-Astro-COLIBRI提供了一种快速、定量的贝叶斯分类服务，能够显著减少误分类事件。

Abstract: The surge in publicly available photometric alerts from wide-field surveys requires automated tools for real-time transient classification. We present NMMA--Astro-COLIBRI, an on-demand Bayesian classification service that couples the Nuclear-physics and Multi-Messenger Astrophysics (NMMA) inference framework to the Astro-COLIBRI real-time multi-messenger platform. After the detection of an optical transient, if photometry is available, it is quality-filtered. The filtered photometry is fitted by nested sampling against a user-selected model from a library of eleven supernova templates; results are delivered to every user within minutes. Applying two or more models on the same optical transient, the service reports the corresponding log Bayes factors as a quantitative ranking of competing subtypes.
  We demonstrate the workflow on SN 2021ugl (ZTF21abotose), a Type IIb supernova initially mistaken for a kilonova candidate by automated real-time pipelines, comparing competing supernova and kilonova models. In an early-time configuration using only the first ~ 6 days of photometry in two bands (ZTF g and r), so ten days before spectroscopic confirmation, the empirical Type IIb template recovers the correct classification, favored over both the kilonova template and the kilonova-mimicking shock-cooling model. In the full 47-day, three-band baseline, it again achieves the highest evidence over every competing supernova and kilonova template. These results highlight the importance of a comprehensive supernova template library for kilonova discrimination in the multi-survey era.
