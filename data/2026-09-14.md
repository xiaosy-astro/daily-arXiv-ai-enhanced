### [1] [Long-term X-ray spectral analysis of Cygnus X-1 using AstroSat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12072)
*H Lalthantluanga,Akash Garg,Ranjeev Misra,Vanzarmawii Chhangte,Lalthakimi Zadeng*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 利用AstroSat对Cygnus X-1进行2016-2019长期光谱分析，发现软态需采用热+温双康普顿化模型才能一致地拟合各态光谱，揭示了双康普顿化对宽带辐射的关键作用。

Motivation: 传统单一康普顿化加薄盘模型在软态拟合差且给出非物理的小盘半径，加相对论盘又需异常高的color factor，暗示存在额外康普顿化成分，有必要建立更合理的模型。

Method: 使用AstroSat对黑洞双星Cygnus X-1进行2016-2019年长期光谱分析，覆盖硬态、中间态和软态；比较单康普顿化+热盘+铁线模型、kerrbb相对论盘模型，以及含热（光学薄）和温（光学厚）日冕的双康普顿化模型。

Result: 单康普顿化模型拟合差，R_in<1 R_g；kerrbb虽改善拟合但需f_col~3.35-5.08的高color factor；双康普顿化模型在所有软、硬态下均给出良好一致的拟合。

Conclusion: 双康普顿化在塑造Cygnus X-1宽带辐射中起关键作用，光谱演化支持采用热+温双康普顿化模型。

Abstract: We performed a spectral analysis of long-term observations (2016-2019) of the black hole binary Cygnus X-1 using AstroSat, covering its hard, intermediate, and soft states. During soft states, we found that a typical spectrum fitted with a single Comptonization, a thermal disc, and an iron line yields poor fits and unphysically small disk radii (R_in < 1 R_g). While adding a relativistic disk component (kerrbb) improved the fit but required high color factors (f_col ~ 3.35-5.08), indicating additional Comptonization. We therefore adopted a dual-Comptonization model with hot (optically thin) and warm (optically thick) coronae. This provided consistent good fits across all soft and hard states of Cygnus X-1. The spectral evolution reveals the critical role of dual Comptonization in shaping the broadband emission of Cygnus X-1.
