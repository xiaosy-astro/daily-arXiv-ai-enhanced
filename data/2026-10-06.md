### [1] [Centaurus A: Opportunities to unveil particle acceleration with the CTAO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06550)
*Cainã de Oliveira,Edivaldo Moura Santos*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文评估 CTAO 对近邻射电星系 Centaurus A 伽马射线起源的探测能力，通过模拟观测研究其谱与形态特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Cen A 是多波段观测充分的近邻射电星系，也是已知伽马射线源，但其高能辐射的物理机制（粒子加速与辐射起源）仍不清楚。

Method: 基于 CTAO Alpha 配置的仪器响应函数（IRF）进行模拟观测，对千秒差距尺度喷流和南巨瓣分别探索轻子/强子起源情景，进行谱分析与形态模拟（点源 vs 高斯扩展模型、扩散与丝状模板），采用 t=50、200h 观测时长及 ϑ=20° 天顶角。

Result: 长时观测（200h）可探测延伸至数十 TeV 的硬谱成分；50h 内可分辨喷流上的扩展伽马辐射并区分点源与高斯模型；CTAO-South 对 S1 区域能量超 10 TeV 的强子情景敏感。

Conclusion: CTAO 对 Cen A 的观测有望在揭示射电星系高能辐射与粒子加速机制方面取得决定性进展。

Abstract: As the nearest radio galaxy, Centaurus A (Cen A) has been observed across multiple wavelengths and over a broad range of angular scales, providing valuable insights into the functioning of radio galaxies. However, despite being a known source of gamma rays, the physical mechanisms responsible for these high energy emissions remain unclear. In this study, we explore the capabilities of the Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory (CTAO) to provide insights into the origin of the gamma-ray emission from Cen A. Using simulated observations based on the CTAO Instrument Response Functions (IRF) for the Alpha Configuration of telescopes, we investigate both the spectral and morphological domains to assess the observatory's potential to constrain competing models of particle acceleration in the kiloparsec-scale jet and the Southern Giant Lobe. For the jet, we explore CTAO observations with t=50 and 200h for different leptonic and hadronic origin scenarios for the very-high-energy gamma-ray emission. We show that longer observation campaigns (t=200h) would probe the presence of hard spectral components extending to tens of TeV. Morphological simulations demonstrate that CTAO can resolve the extended gamma-ray emission along the jet, discriminating between point-like and Gaussian spatial models within t=50h, assuming a known background model and an optimistic zenith angle $\vartheta=$20°. Under similar hypotheses, we studied the Giant Lobes evaluating both diffuse and filamentary emission templates for the S1 region detected by Fermi-LAT. We found that CTAO-South can be sensitive to hadronic scenarios with proton energies extending beyond 10 TeV, for the Alpha Configuration and $\vartheta=$20°. Overall, our results indicate that CTAO observations of Cen A can provide a decisive step toward unveiling the mechanisms driving the high-energy emission and particle acceleration in radio galaxies

### [2] [Can Historic Measurements cast light on the Spectral Differences found between the Pierre Auger Observatory and the Telescope Array?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06592)
*Quentin Luce,Alan A. Watson*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 将AGASA、Volcano Ranch和Haverah Park的历史通量数据与Auger报告的能谱进行残差分析并进行能量经验校正后，历史数据在30 EeV以上与Auger测量吻合良好，表明Telescope Array与Auger能谱之间的张力依然存在。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Pierre Auger Observatory与Telescope Array Collaboration报告的能谱存在众所周知的差异，作者希望理解这些差异的来源。

Method: 以Auger Collaboration报告的能谱为参照，研究AGASA、Volcano Ranch和Haverah Park早期测量通量的残差；在踝部之前（预期两半球通量相同）的区域对能量进行经验性调整。

Result: 能量经验调整后，历史数据在30 EeV以上与Pierre Auger Observatory的测量结果良好一致。

Conclusion: Telescope Array与Auger Observatory所测能谱之间的张力仍然存在。

Abstract: The differences between the energy spectra reported by the Pierre Auger Observatory and the Telescope Array Collaboration are well-known. In an effort to understand these differences, we have studied the residuals of the fluxes measured in early work at AGASA, Volcano Ranch and Haverah Park with reference to the spectrum reported by the Auger Collaboration. After an empirical adjustment to the energies in the region before the ankle, where fluxes from the two hemispheres are expected to be the same, it is found that historic data are in good agreement with measurements made at the Pierre Auger Observatory above 30 EeV. Thus, the tensions between the spectra measured by the Telescope Array and the Auger Observatory remain.

</details>


### [3] [Pair echo model of CTAO/LST-1 signal from GRB 221009A](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06633)
*Andrii Neronov,Ievgen Vovk,Teneman Keita,Davide Miceli*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 利用LHAASO与CTAO/LST-1对GRB 221009A的观测数据，将LST-1在爆发后1.33天的信号解释为甚高能γ射线穿越星系际介质产生的"对回波"，从而限制星系际磁场(IGMF)强度为log(B/G)=-16.2±0.7（关联长度>20 kpc）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 甚高能γ射线在穿越星系际介质时会产生延迟的次级"对回波"信号，探测该信号可用于测量宇宙大尺度结构空洞中的星系际磁场(IGMF)，这是一个难以直接观测的物理量。

Method: 结合LHAASO与CTAO/LST-1望远镜对GRB 221009A的观测数据，建立对回波模型进行拟合。若磁场关联长度λ_B>20 kpc，用对回波解释LST-1信号；若λ_B<20 kpc，则磁场强度估计需按√(20 kpc/λ_B)向上修正。

Result: LST-1在爆发后1.33天测得的源信号可与对回波模型相符，对应IGMF强度log(B/G)=-16.2±0.7（关联长度>20 kpc）。对于源自宇宙学相变的短关联长度IGMF，模型给出磁场强度在1e-14–1e-12 G范围。

Conclusion: 所得IGMF参数与所有已知的IGMF限制一致，包括来自活动星系核γ射线观测的下限约束。

Abstract: Interactions of very-high energy gamma-rays during their propagation through the intergalactic medium result in a delayed secondary ``pair echo'' signal. Detection of such pair echo can be used for measurements of intergalactic magnetic fields (IGMF) in the voids of the Large Scale Structure of the Universe. We notice that a combination of data of LHAASO and CTAO/LST-1 telescopes on the gamma-ray burst GRB 221009A is consistent with the pair echo model. The LST-1 measurement of the source signal 1.33 days after the burst can be interpreted as the pair echo produced by very-high-energy gamma-rays propagating through intergalactic medium with magnetic field of the strength log(B/G)=-16.2+/- 0.7, if its correlation length lambda_B is larger than 20 kpc. If the field correlation length is shorter than 20 kpc, the field strength estimate is modified upwards by a square root of (20 kpc / lambda_B). In the specific case of short correlation length IGMF that is relic from cosmological phase transitions, the pair echo model of the LST-1 signal implies the field strength in the 1e-14-1e-12 G range. Such magnetic field parameters are consistent with all known constraints on IGMF, including the lower bounds from gamma-ray observations of active galactic nuclei.

</details>
