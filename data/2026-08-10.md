### [1] [A unified modeling of X-ray and gamma-ray spectro-polarimetric data: the case of Cygnus X-1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06328)
*Tristan Bouchet,Thomas Siegert,Victoria Grinberg,Jérôme Rodriguez,Floriane Cangemi,Philippe Laurent,Joern Wilms*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个通用的宽带谱偏振建模框架，可同时拟合来自不同仪器的谱和偏振数据。将该框架应用于天鹅座X-1在2 keV-2 MeV范围内的观测数据，改进了对软伽马射线硬尾的表征，并约束了光学薄同步加速辐射截断能量为3.9×10² keV。文章还基于磁场螺旋性和多普勒增亮效应提出了偏振失准的新解释。

Motivation: 近期对黑洞X射线双星（如天鹅座X-1）的X射线和软伽马射线谱偏振观测揭示了与现有吸积和高能发射模型相矛盾的差异，需要新的建模框架来解释这些观测结果。

Method: 提出了一个通用的宽带谱偏振建模框架，能够同时拟合来自不同能量带、不同独立仪器的谱和偏振数据。该方法稳健地整合了不同能量分箱和统计显著性的测量，同时考虑模型分量之间与能量相关的偏振混合效应。

Result: 应用于天鹅座X-1在2 keV-2 MeV范围的数据，改进了软伽马射线硬尾的表征，将光学薄同步加速辐射截断能量约束为(3.9^{+0.6}_{-0.5})×10² keV。

Conclusion: 该框架广泛适用于多仪器谱偏振分析。基于约束结果讨论了Bohm扩散和同步冷却背景下非热电子加速的含义，并提出基于磁场螺旋性和多普勒增亮的偏振失准新解释。

Abstract: Recent X-ray and soft gamma-ray spectro-polarimetric observations of black hole X-ray binaries, such as Cygnus X-1, reveal discrepancies that challenge current models of accretion and high-energy emission. We present a general framework for broadband spectro-polarimetric modeling that simultaneously fits spectral and polarization data from independent instruments across different energy bands. The method robustly combines measurements with different energy binning and statistical significance while accounting for energy-dependent polarization mixing between model components. Applied to Cygnus X-1 over the 2 keV-2 MeV range, it provides an improved characterization of the soft gamma-ray hard-tail and constrains the optically thin synchrotron cutoff energy to $(3.9^{+0.6}_{-0.5}) \times 10^{2}$ keV. We discuss the implications for non-thermal electron acceleration in the context of Bohm diffusion and synchrotron cooling, and propose a new explanation for the observed polarization misalignment based on magnetic field helicity and Doppler boosting. The framework is broadly applicable to multi-instrument spectro-polarimetric analyses.
