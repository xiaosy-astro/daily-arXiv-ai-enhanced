### [1] [Resistive alignment of a black-hole magnetosphere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13288)
*Ruben Lier,Oliver Porth*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文在电阻相对论磁流体力学框架下建立解析理论，解释了黑洞磁层中磁轴与自旋轴趋于对齐的物理起源，即Blandford-Znajek解的二阶自旋效应结合电阻耗散，并用数值模拟验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 此前数值模拟显示黑洞磁层会快速演化为磁轴与自旋轴对齐，但其物理机制一直不清楚，本文旨在给出理论解释。

Method: 采用慢自旋的Blandford-Znajek单极子解作为外部磁场，将感应方程约化为电流片倾角的弛豫方程，作为对齐的解析理论，并用电阻GRMHD模拟在不同自旋和电阻率下检验。

Result: 分析表明对齐源于BZ解中的二阶自旋效应与显式电阻耗散的结合；模拟测得晚期慢衰减与解析预测定量一致。

Conclusion: 磁轴-自旋轴对齐机制得到解释并获数值验证，存在一个电阻与自旋区间使解析与模拟结果定量吻合。

Abstract: Recent numerical simulations have shown that black-hole magnetospheres evolve rapidly toward alignment between the magnetic axis and spin axis but the physical origin of this process has remained unclear. We develop an analytic theory of this alignment within resistive relativistic magnetohydrodynamics. Taking the slow-spin Blandford-Znajek monopole solution as the exterior magnetic field, we reduce the induction equation to a relaxation equation for the current-sheet inclination angle. Our analysis shows that alignment results from second-order spin effects in the Blandford-Znajek solution combined with explicit resistive dissipation. We test this prediction using resistive-GRMHD simulations at several spins and resistivities and find a regime in which the measured late-time secular decay agrees quantitatively with the analytic prediction.
